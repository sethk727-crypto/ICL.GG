Objections, handled

Straight answers

Everything else lives in the UX PRD.

How do you detect smurfs?

Ranks are pulled server-side from Riot, Steam, and Chess.com via OAuth — never self-reported. Fresh accounts with veteran performance curves get flagged, and every player carries a verified match history badge into the lobby.

What anti-cheat systems do you integrate with?

We run a background handshake against Riot Vanguard and Valve Anti-Cheat records at check-in and issue a green “Cleared” trust badge before you can enter a verified bracket.

Are prize pools and wallets secure?

Entry fees sit in Stripe-backed escrow until results are confirmed, then pay out automatically. KYC applies to staked brackets only — free events never ask for payment details.

What is the fair-play score?

A reliability rating that rises when you show up on time and finish your series, and falls on no-shows and abandons. Low scores restrict entry to staked events; consistent teams get priority seeding.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — brackets, lobbies, and stream viewing are fully responsive. Dual-cast theater stacks vertically on small screens, and PiP docks to the bottom of the viewport.

Can a league turn into a tournament?

Yes. Events are format-agnostic: run a round-robin regular season, then roll the standings straight into a single- or double-elimination playoff bracket — same event, same rosters, same wallet.
